Our Services

Solar-first EPC for homes, businesses and farms — with full electrical contracting on the same site. One accountable team for power generation, distribution and consumption.

Solar Services

Our flagship offering. End-to-end solar EPC with high-efficiency modules and 25-year performance warranty on every system.

Residential Rooftop Solar
1 kW – 10 kW grid-tied systems. Eligible for up to ₹1,08,000 central subsidy under PM Surya Ghar Yojana.
Commercial & Industrial Solar
10 kW – 1 MW rooftop & ground-mount plants for factories, offices, hospitals & warehouses. AD & GST benefits applicable.
Off-Grid & Hybrid Systems
Solar + lithium battery backup. Day-night solar power even when the grid fails — perfect for villages, farms & remote sites.
Solar Water Pumps
1 HP – 10 HP solar irrigation pumps under PM-KUSUM Component-B. Up to 60% central + state subsidy for farmers.
Solar + EV Charging
Pair rooftop solar with a home or workplace EV charger. Drive on sunshine — combined PM Surya Ghar + EV incentives where applicable.
Solar O&M & Cleaning
Annual maintenance, robotic & manual cleaning, thermal imaging, inverter health checks & remote monitoring.
Subsidy & DISCOM Paperwork
PM Surya Ghar registration, DISCOM net-metering filings, structural approvals & DBT subsidy follow-up — done for you.
Energy Audits & Sizing
12-month bill analysis, load profiling, shading study & ROI report — so your system is sized right, not oversold.
Solar Street & Outdoor Lights
All-in-one solar street lights for societies, gated communities, schools & rural roads. Maintenance-free LED with smart sensors.

Solar System Types

  • On-Grid (Grid-Tied)
  • Net-metering with DISCOM
  • Lowest cost / kW
  • Eligible for PM Surya Ghar subsidy
  • Best for cities & reliable supply
  • Off-Grid (Battery)
  • Lithium-ion or lead-acid bank
  • Independent of utility grid
  • Best for remote sites & farms
  • Day & night power autonomy
  • Hybrid (Solar + Battery + Grid)
  • Smart inverter switches automatically
  • Backup during outages
  • Net-metering compatible
  • Best for cities with frequent cuts
