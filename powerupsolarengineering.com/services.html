Our Services

Comprehensive Solar EPC solutions for homes, businesses, flour mills and commercial buildings across Uttar Pradesh. High-efficiency systems backed by 25-year performance warranties.

Solar Solutions

End-to-end solar EPC engineering — from 3D shadow analysis to DISCOM net-metering and subsidy disbursement.

Residential Rooftop Solar
1 kW – 10 kW grid-tied systems. Eligible for up to ₹1,08,000* central subsidy under PM Surya Ghar Yojana.
Commercial & Industrial Solar
10 kW – 1 MW rooftop & ground-mount plants for factories, offices, hospitals & warehouses. AD & GST benefits applicable.
Off-Grid Solar Systems
Stand-alone solar power with high-capacity battery bank. Complete energy independence for remote sites, farms & homes with unreliable grid.
Hybrid Solar Systems
Solar + grid + battery backup. Day-night solar power with seamless net-metering grid sync and uninterrupted emergency power.
Solar Atta Chakki
High-yield solar-powered flour mills & agro-milling setups. Run directly on solar VFD drives with zero diesel costs.
Solar O&M & Cleaning
Annual maintenance, robotic & manual cleaning, thermal imaging, inverter health checks & remote monitoring.

Solar System Types

On-Grid (Grid-Tied)

  • Net-metering with DISCOM
  • Lowest cost / kW
  • Eligible for PM Surya Ghar subsidy
  • Best for cities & reliable supply

Off-Grid (Battery)

  • Lithium-ion or lead-acid bank
  • Independent of utility grid
  • Best for remote sites & farms
  • Day & night power autonomy

Hybrid (Solar + Battery + Grid)

  • Smart inverter switches automatically
  • Backup during outages
  • Net-metering compatible
  • Best for cities with frequent cuts
