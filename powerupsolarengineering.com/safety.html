Solar & Electrical Safety Standards

At Liana Solar, safety is non-negotiable. Our engineers follow strict MNRE, CEA and OSHA safety protocols to protect workers, homeowners, and power equipment.

Liana Solar Safety Protocols

  • Certified PPE — Helmets, insulated gloves, anti-skid boots & safety harnesses
  • MNRE & CEA Compliance — All installations adhere to national electrical code
  • Hot-Dip Galvanised Mounting — Certified to withstand 150+ km/h wind speeds
  • Dual Chemical Earthing — Dedicated copper-bonded pits for AC & DC surge protection

  • IP65 / IP66 Weatherproofing — All junction boxes and inverters sealed against rain
  • Anti-Islanding Protection — 100% compliant with UPPCL DISCOM grid safety
