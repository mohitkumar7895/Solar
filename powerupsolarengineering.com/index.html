About Us

A solar EPC company powering homes, factories and commercial buildings across Uttar Pradesh.

Liana Solar designs, supplies and installs end-to-end solar power systems — from a 2 kW rooftop on your home to a 1 MW C&I plant across Uttar Pradesh. We are an MNRE-empanelled installer. Every system we deliver is backed by high-efficiency, made-in-India panels with a 25-year module warranty.

From precision 3D shadow analysis and Tier-1 Mono PERC/TopCon modules to DISCOM net-metering liaison and direct subsidy disbursement, our in-house engineers manage every stage of your solar journey.

  • Solar EPC — rooftop, off-grid, hybrid, solar chakki & C&I plants
  • Solar Chakki — high-efficiency solar atta chakki & flour milling systems
  • Subsidy & net-metering — PM Surya Ghar paperwork done for you
  • Tier-1 Modules — 25-year linear performance warranty
  • O&M Support — solar plant performance monitoring & AMC
  • Local presence — office in Uttar Pradesh, India

Government Subsidy on Your Roof

Under PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, the central government will pay a chunk of your solar bill — and we will handle the paperwork.

2 kW System

Up to ₹90,000* total subsidy (₹60,000* CFA + ₹30,000* UP). Ideal for small homes using 150–300+ units / month.

3 kW System

Up to ₹1,08,000* total subsidy (₹78,000* CFA + ₹30,000* UP). Suits mid-size homes using 300–500+ units / month.

5 kW & above

Up to ₹1,08,000* total subsidy (cap). Ideal for homes using 450–750+ units / month.
