About Us

Liana Solar is a solar-first EPC company serving Andhra Pradesh and Telangana from offices in Rajahmundry and Hyderabad. We are locally owned and operated, MNRE-empanelled, and an associate channel of Tata Power Solar — India's #1 rooftop solar EPC. Our mission is simple: every roof we touch should generate clean power, every paisa of subsidy our clients are entitled to should reach their bank account, and every installation should still be running strong 25 years from now.

Our roots are in electrical contracting, and that legacy is core to what makes our solar work different. The same engineers who design your solar plant also handle your switchboard upgrade, your earthing, your surge protection and your wiring. No sub-contracted hand-offs. No "call the electrician" once the panels are up. One accountable team for power generation, distribution and consumption.

Whether you are a homeowner looking to claim the PM Surya Ghar subsidy, a factory owner chasing accelerated depreciation on a 500 kW rooftop, a farmer eligible for PM-KUSUM, or a school board electrifying a campus — we will design the right system, file the right paperwork, install it cleanly, and stand behind it for the long haul. Call us for a free site survey and a transparent quote.

Associate Channel — Tata Power Solar

Tata Power Solar has solarised 1.5 lakh+ rooftops, deployed 3 GW+ of capacity and reaches 700+ cities through 575+ channel partners. As a Tata Power Solar associate, every kilowatt we install carries the Tata trust mark — Tier-1 modules, 25-year module warranty, lifetime service support, and easy financing through Tata Power's bank tie-ups.
