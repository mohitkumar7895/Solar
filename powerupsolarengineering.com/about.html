About Us

Liana Solar is a solar EPC company serving Uttar Pradesh. We are locally owned and operated, MNRE-empanelled, and dedicated to delivering high-quality rooftop solar systems. Our mission is simple: every roof we touch should generate clean power, every paisa of subsidy our clients are entitled to should reach their bank account, and every installation should still be running strong 25 years from now.

Our engineering precision and commitment to quality is what makes our solar work stand out. From precision shadow analysis and heavy-duty structural engineering to seamless DISCOM net-metering approvals and direct DBT subsidy disbursement, our in-house certified engineers manage every step with zero sub-contracting.

Whether you are a homeowner looking to claim the PM Surya Ghar subsidy, a factory owner chasing accelerated depreciation on a 500 kW rooftop, an entrepreneur setting up a solar flour mill, or a school board electrifying a campus — we will design the right system, file the right paperwork, install it cleanly, and stand behind it for the long haul. Call us for a free site survey and a transparent quote.
