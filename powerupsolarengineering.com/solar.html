Pick the Right System Type

On-Grid (Grid-Tied)
Best for cities with reliable grid. Net-metering exports surplus power back to DISCOM, lowering your bill to near zero. Eligible for PM Surya Ghar subsidy.
Off-Grid (Battery)
Independent of the grid — runs entirely on solar plus a lithium-ion or lead-acid battery bank. Perfect for villages, farms, towers and remote sites.
Hybrid (Solar + Battery + Grid)
Best of both worlds: solar by day, battery backup at night, grid as a fallback. Rides through power cuts seamlessly while still saving on your bill.

Government Schemes & Subsidies

India is incentivising every kilowatt of rooftop solar. Here is the money on the table — and we will help you claim it.

1. PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana (Central Govt. — flagship scheme)

Launched 13 Feb 2024 by the Ministry of New & Renewable Energy, this is the largest residential rooftop solar scheme in India, targeting 1 crore households by March 2027. Beneficiaries get up to 300 units of free electricity every month.

System Size Central Subsidy (CFA) Suitable For
1 kW Up to ₹30,000 Small homes, < 150 units / month
2 kW Up to ₹60,000 Mid-size homes, 150–300 units / month
3 kW & above Up to ₹78,000 (cap) Large homes, > 300 units / month

How we help: we register you on pmsuryaghar.gov.in, file the application with your DISCOM, install a vendor-approved system, complete the inspection, and ensure the subsidy is credited via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to your bank account.

2. Andhra Pradesh — Solar Subsidy & Net Metering

  • Residents eligible for up to ₹18,000 / kW additional state-level support on residential rooftops.
  • 100% subsidy for SC/ST beneficiaries; up to 40% for other eligible social categories.
  • Net-metering through APEPDCL & APSPDCL — sell surplus units back to the grid for bill credit.
  • Maximum net-metering capacity: 1 MWp per consumer.

3. Telangana — Domestic Sector Subsidy

  • 40% of benchmark cost for systems from 1 kW to 3 kW.
  • 20% additional subsidy for systems above 3 kW up to 10 kW.
  • 3 kW on-grid systems eligible for the central ₹78,000 support combined with state benefits.
  • Net-metering through TGSPDCL/TGNPDCL with no cross-subsidy or wheeling charges for residential users.

4. PM-KUSUM (for Farmers)

  • Component A: grid-connected solar plants (500 kW – 2 MW) on barren / fallow land.
  • Component B: stand-alone solar pumps (1 HP – 10 HP) — up to 60% central + state subsidy, farmer pays only ~40%.
  • Component C: solarisation of existing grid-connected agricultural pumps.

5. C&I Tax Benefits

  • Accelerated Depreciation of 40% in Year 1 for businesses installing rooftop solar.
  • GST input credit on equipment for registered businesses.
  • Eligible for Group Captive & Open Access arrangements for large industrial users.
